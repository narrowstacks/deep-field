# Deep Field: website copy

## Name

Deep Field (two words, both capitalized). The app is "Deep Field", not "DeepField"; `DeepField`
is only the binary's name.

## Taglines

Pick one; the first is the one on the banner.

1. Animated space wallpapers for macOS.
2. A black hole on your desktop.
3. Real physics, rendered like an old screen.
4. Lensed light, dithered.
5. Your desktop, a few light-years out.

## Short description (one sentence)

Deep Field is a menu bar app that turns your Mac's desktop into an animated, pixel-dithered
space scene: a lensed black hole, a wormhole, a galaxy, a pulsar and more.

## Description (three sentences)

Deep Field puts an animated space scene behind your windows: a black hole with a lensed
accretion disk, a wormhole, binary and trinary stars, five kinds of galaxy, a pulsar, a comet,
or the surface of a star. Each one is built on real physics and drawn as chunky, ordered-dither
pixel art, with rare events like supernovae, flares and comets drifting through the sky. It
traces light once, renders at a third of screen resolution and stops when the desktop is
covered, so it stays light on the battery.

## Feature bullets

- Nine scenes, each with its own parameters, colors, events and camera.
- Light bent by a Schwarzschild black hole and an Ellis wormhole, traced on the GPU.
- Galaxies that rotate on a real rotation curve, with or without dark matter.
- Supernovae, comets, flares and coronal mass ejections, a few per hour.
- Six palettes, or pick every color yourself.
- Pixel size, color levels, dither pattern, bloom, vignette and tone controls.
- A matching still on the lock and login screens.
- iPhone wallpaper export as a still or a Live Photo.
- Renders about 1/9 of the pixels at 24 fps, and pauses when you can't see it.
- Runs on macOS 14 or later, from the menu bar.
